## Leads

**1. Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen** — The leaders of the United
States, China and Russia met at Shenzhen's Kylin Villa on the sidelines
of the APEC summit, the first three-way meeting of the three powers.
Trump and Xi held a bilateral at 12:45 Shenzhen time, followed by the
trilateral at about 15:30 KST. The agenda covered nuclear arms control
(the New START treaty expired in February 2026), the Iran war and trade;
Ukraine was reportedly off the US agenda and no agreement was expected.
— Reuters, Le Monde, AJP and the New York Times, via 19 Nov news
round-ups.

**2. Israeli tanks cross Gaza ceasefire line** — Israeli tanks and
ground forces crossed the "Yellow Line" ceasefire line in central Gaza,
advancing east of the Nuseirat and Bureij camps onto the Salah al-Din
road — the first ground advance since the October 2025 truce. The IDF
said it was dismantling a tunnel that threatened its troops; Hamas
called the move a flagrant violation, at least three people were killed
in strikes, and the Board of Peace warned the truce could collapse.
— Al Jazeera and wire reporting, 19 Nov.

**3. Jeffries nominated for Speaker, 251–11** — US House Democrats
nominated Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker in a closed-door vote of 251–11
on Wednesday 18 November. Jeffries is poised to become the first Black
Speaker; Democrats are projected to hold 259 House seats, their largest
majority in 35 years. The floor vote falls on 3 January 2027 and needs
218 votes; Katherine Clark is set to be the first woman Majority
Leader.
— AP and CNN, 18–19 Nov.

## Grid stories (4–13)

**4. Anthropic IPO is biggest ever: $81.3bn** — Anthropic priced its
IPO at $83 a share, above the $70–78 range, selling 980 million shares
to raise $81.3 billion — the largest IPO in history, eclipsing SpaceX —
at a fully diluted valuation of about $1.5 trillion. It debuted on the
Nasdaq on 19 November under the ticker ANT, alongside a $26 billion
private placement (Google, Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft,
TSMC). Amazon holds about 14.7% and Google about 13.7% after the IPO.
— Financial and wire reporting, 19 Nov.

**5. Crimea bridge hit again; Odesa toll rises** — Ukraine struck the

also claimed strikes on a bridge near Stanytsia Luhanska and a repair
facility near Khomutovka.
— Ukrainian General Staff statements and wire reporting, 19 Nov.

**7. Voyager 1 passes one light-day** — NASA's Voyager 1 reached one
light-day from Earth on Wednesday 18 November 2026 at 05:16 EST —
16.1 billion miles (25.9 billion km) out, the first human-made object
to do so. Its signals now take a full 24 hours to arrive. NASA's JPL
marked the milestone with a public discussion on 19 November.
— NASA JPL, Smithsonian Magazine and ScienceAlert, 18–19 Nov.

**8. Van Gogh sells for record $190.4m** — Van Gogh's *Châtaigniers en
fleurs* (1890) sold at Sotheby's New York on Wednesday 18 November for
$190.4 million with fees (hammer $165 million, to dealer Bill
Acquavella), smashing the previous Van Gogh record of $117.2 million.
The sale followed a settlement with the heirs of Jewish publisher
Samuel Fischer over the painting's Nazi-era 1936 sale.
— Sotheby's results and arts reporting, 18–19 Nov.

**9. COP31 deadlocked with two days left** — The COP31 climate summit

fossil fuels; Türkiye's "35 by 35" electrification pledge — electricity
at 35% of final energy by 2035 — was backed by 75 countries; and a
draft adaptation-finance option of $120 billion a year by 2035 was on
the table.
